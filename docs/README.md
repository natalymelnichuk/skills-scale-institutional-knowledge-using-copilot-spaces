# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation. This folder contains comprehensive guides and processes used by OctoAcme to manage projects from initiation through retrospective.

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a structured, customer-centric project management framework built on five key lifecycle phases: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective. The approach emphasizes iterative delivery of small, testable increments with clear ownership and data-driven decision-making. Projects begin with lightweight initiation activities—creating a Project One-pager that defines the problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and initial timeline. Once approved by sponsors, teams move into detailed planning, where work is broken down into prioritized backlog items with acceptance criteria, dependencies are mapped, and release milestones are established.

OctoAcme organizes teams around three core roles: Project Managers (who coordinate delivery, manage schedules and risks, and facilitate communications), Product Managers (who define customer value, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes), and Developers (who implement features and collaborate on quality). Weekly syncs between the PM and Product Manager drive strategic alignment, while twice-weekly standups keep the delivery team synchronized on progress and blockers. Stakeholder communication follows a consistent cadence with monthly updates and a single source of truth for project status. Clear escalation paths—from team-level to PM to Product Lead to Sponsor—ensure issues are resolved at the appropriate level without delays.

Execution at OctoAcme is grounded in quality and visibility through a defined workflow using project boards with clear stages (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done) and disciplined pull request practices. Quality assurance is built into the process through multiple layers: unit tests for new logic, integration and end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning in the CI pipeline, and manual QA for feature acceptance when needed. The team maintains a Risk Register throughout execution to identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor risks, with weekly reviews at team syncs. Metrics and dashboards track velocity, burndown, and success metrics from the Project One-pager, enabling data-informed course corrections.

Finally, OctoAcme embeds continuous improvement into its culture through structured retrospectives held after each sprint, release, or significant milestone. These sessions follow a consistent format (What went well, What could be improved, and Action items with clear owners and due dates) and are timeboxed to encourage focused, candid feedback. Action items are tracked in the project backlog and reviewed during weekly PM syncs to ensure improvements are implemented and measured for impact. This commitment to learning and iterative refinement—combined with the psychological safety emphasized in core principles—creates a culture where teams systematically improve their processes and delivery outcomes over time.
